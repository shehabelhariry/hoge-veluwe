Getting there from Amsterdam

No car needed — it's a train and a bus, about two hours door-to-gate. Tap a contactless bank card (OVpay) or an OV-chipkaart to ride both; no paper tickets required.

The route · roughly 2 hours

Amsterdam Centraal

Trains east leave several times an hour — grab a coffee for the platform and pick the next Intercity.

Train · Intercity ≈ 1 h 05

Direct to Ede-Wageningen. Watch the flat Randstad give way to forest and heather as you roll onto the Veluwe. Apeldoorn and Arnhem Centraal also work as gateway stations if the timing suits you better.

Ede-Wageningen

Your transfer point. Follow the signs to the bus bays right outside the station entrance.

Bus · line 106 / 108 ≈ 35 min

In summer a direct bus 106 often runs from the station straight to the park and museum on weekends — and today's a Saturday, so you may be in luck. Otherwise take bus 108 to Otterlo, Rotonde and change to bus 106. Always worth a glance at the 9292 travel planner first.

De Hoge Veluwe · Otterlo entrance

You've arrived. Buy your park tickets online in advance — the bus fare doesn't include entry — then grab a free White Bike and go.

Your 11:00 train — the verdict

Fine for a relaxed Saturday — just a touch late.

An 11:00 from Amsterdam Centraal puts you at the gate around 13:00. That's ample for the Kröller-Müller Museum (open till 17:00), a White-Bike loop, and the golden late-afternoon light when the deer come out. The catch: you'll miss the calm dawn wildlife window, and the museum closes at 17:00. If spotting animals is the whole point, an earlier train (≈ 09:00) is the smarter call. Either way, check the last bus back from Otterlo before you settle in — evening services thin out fast.
